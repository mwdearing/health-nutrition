# Journal, entry detail and Library

## Screens
- **Journal** lists active entries by local day, in the time zone of each entry's own time. Days are newest first, and entries are newest first within a day. Deleted entries are hidden.
  - **The day header** is a card at the top of each day. It shows the day title, the day's energy as a figure such as "1240 kcal" when the day's total is known (an unknown total shows nothing, never 0), and up to three goal bars: one for each stored goal, in the order the goals were stored, with water left out. A goal whose nutrient has no amount that day still gets a bar, and its text is one of two states. "Can't total yet" when the day has logged food or drink whose amount for that nutrient is unknown. "Nothing logged yet" when the day has no food or drink entry, where water and supplements do not count. The day's summary line is still computed by the model but is no longer drawn.
  - **Entries are grouped by meal** under the header, as small headings: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snack in that order, then "Other" last. "Other" holds an entry with no meal, a meal this build does not name, and water with no meal. A meal with no entries is left out. Each entry still names its meal on its detail line, and it opens in the entry screen.
  - **Old days collapse.** A day more than seven days before today, read in the entry's own zone, starts collapsed to one button that reads "9 entries" (or "1 entry") with the hint "Shows this day's entries". A tap opens the day, and the hint then reads "Hides this day's entries" while a tap on the same button collapses it again. Which days are open is kept in memory only and is not stored. A day within the last seven days always shows its entries.
  - **An entry that cannot be read** is left out, and a note says how many were left out: "1 entry can't be shown because its saved time or record can't be read." For more than one, the note reads "3 entries can't be shown because their saved time or record can't be read." An entry whose stored time zone is invalid or whose record cannot be read is never placed on a guessed day. A failed read of the whole journal shows "Could not read the journal." instead.
  - **The empty journal.** When a load finishes with no day and no unreadable entry, the screen shows "Your journal is empty" with the message "Everything you log is listed here by day." It shows an "Add food or drink" button only when the screen is given an add action.
  - **Later placeholders.** A "Jump to date" row at the top of the list and an "Add to this day" row in each day header are disabled, carry a Later badge, and read "Not available yet" to a screen reader. Neither does anything yet.
  - **Not yet.** The Journal has no week summary and no search; neither is shown as a placeholder.
- **Entry detail** is read first, under the title "Entry". From the top:
  - A failed save shows a notice at the top: "Couldn't save. Your previous amount is kept." An entry that no longer exists shows an empty state, "This entry is no longer available.", with a Close button.
  - Header: the product's name, or the first component's name when there is no product, or "Entry". The brand follows when the product has one, then a chip for a drink or a supplement.
  - Amounts: one field per component in its stored unit, with the same amount in the reader's unit beneath when there is one. A missing amount shows "unknown", never 0. A Meal row shows the meal, or "Not set", with a Later badge, because editing the meal is not built yet.
  - **This entry adds** shows the standard nutrients and the tracked nutrients (potassium, sodium, protein and fiber by default) scaled to the amount this entry logged. A product stated per 100 g with 13 g of protein, with 50 g logged, reads "6.5 g". A standard nutrient the label does not state reads "Not on the label", never 0. A tracked nutrient the label does not state is left out. If the logged amount cannot be matched to the label's basis, a nutrient the label states reads "Can't be worked out for this amount" and one it does not state still reads "Not on the label". A nutrient the product marks as not applicable reads "Does not apply", as in the Add preview, whether or not the amount can be scaled. An entry with no product snapshot has no section.
  - **All N values** opens the rest of the label's values, unscaled and sorted by name. N counts every value the label states. Compounds a label scan captured appear under "Also on the label" and are not repeated in the main list.
  - **Where this came from** shows one line: "Typed in · no nutrition values" (no product, or a manual one), "Label scan · <basis>", "Recipe · <product name>", or "Barcode lookup · values <basis>", for example "Barcode lookup · values per 100 g". That line carries a Later badge. Under it, rows read from the stored snapshot: for a barcode lookup, Input "Barcode lookup", Barcode, Catalog version and Basis; for a label scan, Input "Label scan" and Basis (a catalog version of "unknown" is left out, never shown); for a recipe, Input "Recipe", Recipe and Version; for a typed entry, Input "Typed in"; for an entry with no snapshot, Source "No source recorded". A label scan identifier is not recorded. A typed entry also shows a disabled "Add values from a label" placeholder.
  - **Sent to** has one row per destination, "Apple Health" and "HealthRelay". Each row has an icon and one of five words: "Waiting to send", "Sending", "Sent", "Needs attention" or "Not connected". A row that needs attention carries a disabled "Try again" placeholder.
  - **Changes** lists the entry's changes, newest first, each with its date and time. The first is "Logged", and revision 1 never shows a note. A later change reads "Amount changed", "Time corrected", "Amount changed and time corrected", or "Changed" when neither the amounts nor a recorded time differ from the revision before. A reason is shown under the verb only when the person wrote one. The default reason "Edited", the stored "created" on revision 1, the automatic "Time corrected" and a reason that repeats the verb are not shown.
  - Actions: "Log again" adds a new entry (see Repeat semantics). "Favorite" is a disabled placeholder with a Later badge. "Delete entry" asks for confirmation first.
  - Save appears in the toolbar only after a change: an amount that differs from the one the entry states, or a time that differs from the stored one. Once something has changed, an "Add a note" disclosure appears; expanded, it holds an "Optional note" field that starts as "Edited", which is recorded as no note. Saving writes one new revision and keeps the old one, then the screen closes. The amounts and the time are always shown and editable; only Save and the note wait for a change.
  - The placeholders are disabled and marked Later: the Meal row, the source line, "Add values from a label", "Try again" and "Favorite".
- **When** is the entry's time, in a date picker bounded to now: nobody has eaten anything in the future, and a logged time is corrected backwards. The picker is bound to the entry's **stored** time zone, so what it shows and what it writes is the wall clock the entry states rather than the same instant read in the device's zone; a stored identifier that does not resolve falls back to the current zone. Saving goes through the same one `edit` call as an amount, so the correction is a new revision with the previous one kept, the journal then lists the entry under the corrected day, and the Changes list shows the correction as a row like any other. A change of time alone is recorded as "Time corrected" unless a reason was written; a written reason is kept as written. A save that changes the amounts **and** the time is an ordinary edit and is recorded as one, since the time was not the only thing that moved. An edit that does not change the time writes no correction at all.
- Each revision keeps the instant it was written with, so a queued revision that is delivered after the entry's time has been corrected still describes the instant it was written for, rather than the corrected one. See [Journal delivery](relay-delivery.md) and [HealthKit writer](healthkit-writer.md).
- **Edit** parses amounts with the fixed POSIX parser (digits and one point, no locale, no sign). Invalid text gives a field error and writes nothing; editing that field clears its error, so the message never outlives the text it was about. A valid save makes exactly one `edit` call and a new revision; if the write fails the previous revision stays and the screen shows "Couldn't save. Your previous amount is kept." **The parser gates what was typed, not what is stored**: a field still reading exactly the stored amount is carried through as the stored component, without being re-parsed. A zero amount is a fact the store, the export, the importer and the encoder all accept but the parser refuses, so an entry holding one can still be saved — a time correction above all, which changes no amount.
- **Delete** asks for confirmation, then makes one `delete` call. The entry is hidden, delete operations are queued and the history is kept.
- **Library** opens with a disabled "Search the Library" row that carries a Later badge, then a segmented control with four segments: **Favorites**, **Recent**, **Foods** and **Recipes**. It starts on Favorites. Each item row shows the title, the amounts and the meal, with a "Supplement" mark for a supplement, a trailing **Add** capsule and a star.
  - **A tap on an item opens the Add details form**, prefilled with the item's amounts and, when the item's meal is one the app names (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snack), with that meal. A stored meal the app does not name is not carried over. The row is labeled "<title>, opens details" with the hint "Opens details without adding an entry". Nothing is logged until Save on that form.
  - **An item that cannot be opened** (it has no amounts, or its saved product is no longer available) opens nothing. The Library then shows "This item can't be opened. Its saved product is no longer available." as a footnote at the foot of the list, under the segment's rows or empty state.
  - **The trailing Add logs at once.** It repeats the item as a new entry now (the same single `create` as Repeat, see below) and shows an **Undo** toast at the bottom of the screen for ten seconds. Undo deletes exactly the entry that Add wrote; like Delete, the entry is hidden and its history kept. Starting another quick add clears the previous Undo, so Undo only ever refers to the entry just added. The Undo offer exists on the Library tab only: it is cleared when you leave the tab, and it is cleared when the ten seconds run out. An item with no amounts logs nothing and offers no Undo.
  - **The star** marks a favorite. A filled star removes the stored favorite copy; an empty star saves one. The star works in both the Favorites and the Recent segment.
  - **Favorites** lists the stored favorites. When it is empty it reads "No favorites yet" with the message "Star anything you log often." and a star symbol.
  - **Recent** lists the recent items. When it is empty it reads "Nothing logged yet" with the message "Things you log will show up here." and a clock symbol.
  - **Foods** is a placeholder: it reads "Foods" with the message "Foods you've scanned will be kept here so you can log them again.", a barcode symbol and a Later badge, and reads "Not available yet" to a screen reader.
  - **Recipes** shows an "Open recipes" row. That row opens the recipes sheet exactly as before; the recipe list is not moved into this screen. Where the screen is given no recipes action, the segment reads "No recipes yet" with the message "Recipes you write are kept here." and a book symbol.
  - **Add's "From Library" picker** has its own segmented control with three segments: **Favorites**, **Recent** and **Recipes**, and it opens on Recent. Favorites and Recent each show the same item list as the Library's pick mode: one section with the matching rows, each with its star, but no Add capsule and no search row. A tap on an item goes to the Add details form, and nothing is logged there until Save. An empty section shows only its message line. If that form cannot be built, Add shows "Could not open this item. Its saved product may no longer be available." instead of the Library's message. Recipes shows the personal recipe list instead, and a tap on a recipe opens that recipe. The picker does not offer Foods.
  - **The meal is part of an item's identity**: the same product at breakfast and at dinner is two recents and two favorites, because they are two entries a person adds again separately. The key spells every part out with a length prefix — snapshot, category, component names and meal — so no join character and no meal can make two entries stand for one.

## Repeat semantics
Repeat makes one `create` call for a NEW intake: new lowercase UUID, `occurredAt` is now, the current time zone, the same category and meal, the same components (names, amounts, units) and the same product snapshot if there was one. The original entry is not touched. The entry screen's "Log again" button makes this call; on success the screen closes. The Library's trailing Add makes the same call and stays on the Library, with Undo on offer.

In Add, a single-component Library pick opens Details before Save. Keeping the product's name,
brand and kind preserves its stored snapshot identity; editing any of those creates a derived
snapshot with the original nutrient values and provenance. Combined entries keep a separate,
per-serving snapshot. Add home's Scan label starts a fresh form; scanning from Details or the
barcode screen keeps that screen's form. Details offers Scan label only on a device that can scan.

Scanning a label from Library Details preserves the chosen name and brand while replacing the
nutrient values. "This adds" scales stated nutrients to the typed amount, including potassium
when stated; a nutrient the product does not state is omitted, standard ones included, while a
"does not apply" value is still shown. Products with no known nutrition values show
the existing no-stated-nutrients sentence instead of a preview of unknown rows. Canonical journal
keys and label aliases are excluded from "Also on the label", leaving only additional compounds.

## Favorites and recents
- **Recents** are derived, not stored: the 20 most recent distinct active entries, distinct by product snapshot id **and the meal**, otherwise by category, component names and the meal.
- **Favorites** are stored templates (display name, category, components as exact decimal text plus unit symbol, optional product snapshot id) in their own store file next to the journal file. A favorite is a copy, not a link, so deleting an entry never removes a favorite.

## Entry points
Every tab (Today, Journal, Library) has a gear at the top right that opens **Settings**, and the same "Add food or drink" capsule above the tab bar. Today's rows open the same entry screen the Journal opens, in a sheet with a navigation bar and a Close button. Settings (`AppSettingsView`) holds the way in to the daily goals and to the units, data and privacy screen, where the journal can be exported as versioned JSON: see [Journal export](journal-export.md). Today's "Edit goals" link opens Settings straight onto the goals. The Library no longer carries a Connections section, and Today no longer carries Journal and Library links. These are navigation callbacks only; the Today and Add view models keep their roles.

Settings is an interim shell until its own package: besides those two links it lists the features the product intends and this build lacks (Daily prompts, Apple Health, HealthRelay, Community sharing, Keep history for) as disabled rows with a "Later" badge.

## Deferred
Recipes (ingredient lines, yield and per-serving math) are deferred to NC-04b.

## Follow-ups

- `onFromLibrary` on the Add form is the one navigation hook left; the shell supplies it and switches to the Library tab.
- Unknown amount handling: "unknown" is shown only for a NaN amount, an empty component list or a blank name, because `Decimal` cannot otherwise represent a missing amount. Revisit when the model can.
