# Privacy

Health Nutrition stores your food, supplement and water journal on this device. It does not upload the journal as a whole. Some separate actions send specific information to the service needed for that action; this page describes them.

- **Where data lives.** Entries, product snapshots, recipes, favorites, goals, reminders and export files are stored on this device. They are not partitioned by community account: signing in as another person does not create a separate journal or move this data. Your device may also include local data in an iCloud or computer backup, depending on your backup settings.
- **Apple Health and HealthRelay.** Neither connection can be enabled in release builds, and their unavailable controls are not saved as connection settings. Release builds do not read HealthKit data or queue HealthKit writes. Debug builds include a HealthKit test flow and can write test samples; erasing the app's journal does not remove samples already written by a debug build. HealthRelay is unavailable in this version.
- **Barcode lookups.** Typing alone sends nothing. A lookup starts when you submit the field or tap Look up; if you choose Scan, it starts after the camera accepts a barcode. Nothing is sent while the camera is open. The barcode is sent to Open Food Facts to find product facts. If it has no product and you are signed in, the app also asks the community catalog, whether or not Share labels is on. Turning Share labels off stops new label submissions; it does not disable barcode lookup. A signed-out person does not get the authenticated community lookup.
- **Label scanning and sharing.** Nutrition Facts and Supplement Facts are read on the device. Photos are not stored or sent by this app. If you are signed in, have read the sharing disclosure for that account, and Share labels is on, a qualifying label you save can send its product name, brand, barcode, serving text and nutrient amounts to the community catalog. Your personal intake amount, meal, time, notes and photo are not part of that submission.
- **Account.** Accounts are optional and are available only in builds with the project settings. Signing in sends your email address, account ID and optional display name to the project's server. The sharing disclosure is acknowledged separately for each account on this device. Turning sharing off prevents new label submissions; it does not withdraw submissions already sent. Deleting an account removes that account and its submissions, but an independently reviewed catalog label may remain without contributor identity. Your journal and other device data are kept.
- **Email sign-in check.** Asking for an emailed sign-in code may show an hCaptcha human check. Its embedded browser challenge can process browser/device signals and sends a verification result with the sign-in request. It does not receive your journal or label values. Sign in with Apple does not use this email-code check.
- **No telemetry.** The app has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting upload or AI upload.
- **Export and erase.** Export creates a JSON file on this device, and the system share sheet sends it only to the destination you choose. Erase all data removes the app's local journal rows, preferences, local account session and export files it can still reach. It does not delete a server account or shared submissions, remove a debug HealthKit sample, or reach a copy already shared or saved in another app. Row deletion is not a promise of forensic erasure.

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/mwdearing/health-nutrition/issues (never include real health data). Security reports: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
