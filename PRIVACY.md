# Privacy

Health Nutrition stores your food, supplement and water journal on this device. It does not upload the journal as a whole. Some separate actions send specific information to the service needed for that action; this page describes them.

- **Where data lives.** Entries, product snapshots, recipes, favorites, saved meals, your supplement list, goals, reminders and export files are stored on this device. They are not partitioned by community account: signing in as another person does not create a separate journal or move this data. Your device may also include local data in an iCloud or computer backup, depending on your backup settings.
- **Apple Health and HealthRelay.** Apple Health is off until you tap Connect Apple Health in Settings and allow it. After that, nutrients from real logged food and water entries are written to HealthKit on this phone; entries logged before you connect wait on the device and are written once you do, for the nutrient types you allowed. The app never reads other Health data, with one exception you choose: if you tap Show steps, activity and weight on Today and allow it, it reads your steps, active energy and weight to show them there. It also looks up the samples it wrote itself, to replace or remove them when you edit or delete an entry. Nothing read from Health leaves the phone. You can turn writing off per type in the Health app's sharing settings. A debug build's HealthKit screen can also write separate test samples. Erasing the app's journal does not remove HealthKit samples already written; HealthKit has its own store. HealthRelay is unavailable in this version, and its unavailable control is not saved as a connection setting.
- **Barcode lookups.** Typing alone sends nothing. A lookup starts when you submit the field or tap Look up; if you choose Scan, it starts after the camera accepts a barcode. Nothing is sent while the camera is open. The barcode is sent to Open Food Facts to find product facts. If it has no product and you are signed in, the app also asks the community catalog, whether or not Share labels is on. Turning Share labels off stops new label submissions; it does not disable barcode lookup. A signed-out person does not get the authenticated community lookup.
- **Searching Open Food Facts by name.** In Find products, when you submit two or more characters (not while typing), the words you typed are sent to Open Food Facts to find product facts, signed in or not, and nothing else from the device. The screen says so beside the results. Supplement searches are not sent there.
- **Finding shared products.** Searching your own custom products happens on this device. When you are signed in and submit two or more characters in Find products, the words you typed are sent to the project's server with your signed-in account to search shared products, whether or not Share labels is on. A signed-out person does not make this request.
- **Label scanning and sharing.** Nutrition Facts and Supplement Facts are read on the device. Photos are not stored or sent by this app. If you are signed in, have read the sharing disclosure for that account, and Share labels is on, a qualifying label you save can send its product name, brand, barcode, serving text and nutrient amounts to the community catalog. Each submission is linked to your signed-in account on the server. It does not include your journal entries, personal intake amount, meal, time, notes or photo.
- **Sharing a product you create.** Under the same conditions (signed in, disclosure read for that account, Share labels on), saving a new custom product that you entered by hand can send its kind, name, brand, any barcode, serving and each nutrient's name, state and amount to the community catalog, linked to your signed-in account, with the same exclusions. Removing your contribution withdraws it from publication; the server keeps a withdrawn record and receipt linked to your account until that account is deleted.
- **Account.** Accounts are optional and are available only in builds with the project settings. Signing in sends your email address, account ID and optional display name to the project's server. The sharing disclosure is acknowledged separately for each account on this device. Turning sharing off prevents new label submissions; it does not withdraw submissions already sent. Deleting an account removes that account and its submissions, but an independently reviewed catalog label may remain without contributor identity. Your journal and other device data are kept.
- **Email sign-in check.** Asking for an emailed sign-in code may show an hCaptcha human check. Its embedded browser challenge can process browser/device signals and sends a verification result with the sign-in request. In rare cases the challenge may request camera access. It does not receive your journal or label values. Sign in with Apple does not use this email-code check.
- **No telemetry.** The app has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting upload or AI upload.
- **Export and erase.** Export creates a JSON file on this device, and the system share sheet sends it only to the destination you choose. Erase all data removes the app's local journal rows, preferences, local account session and export files it can still reach. It does not delete a server account or shared submissions, remove HealthKit samples already written, or reach a copy already shared or saved in another app. Row deletion is not a promise of forensic erasure.

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/mwdearing/health-nutrition/issues (never include real health data). Security reports: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
